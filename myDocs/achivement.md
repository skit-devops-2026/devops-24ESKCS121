⭐ What Makes StudySphere Different?

Instead of adding flashy AI everywhere, let's add a few features that genuinely improve learning.

1. Smart Study Planner ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

A student enters:

Exam date
Available study hours/day

StudySphere automatically creates a personalized weekly study plan.

This is practical and achievable.

2. AI Lecture Summary ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

After each lecture:

Generate Summary

The AI produces concise revision notes.

Very useful before exams.

3. AI Quiz Generator ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

The student clicks:

Generate Quiz

The AI creates 5–10 questions from the lecture.

Great for self-assessment.

4. Daily Learning Streak ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆

Like GitHub contributions or Duolingo:

🔥 15-Day Streak

This motivates consistent learning.

5. Weekly Learning Progress ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆

Instead of just:

20% completed

Show:

Week 1 ✅

Week 2 ✅

Week 3 🟡

Week 4 🔒

This mirrors structured courses like NPTEL while giving students a clearer roadmap.
